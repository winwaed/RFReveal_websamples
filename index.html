RF Reveal map

Click the map to evaluate a patch at that point using the ticked towers. The result opens in a popup at the point, and each patch's raster stays on the map with a circle at the patch radius.

Evaluate buildings colours every building footprint within the radius of the ticked towers by its coverage; click a building for its figures.

Right-click anywhere for the signal from each ticked tower to that point and the bearing to it: lines are drawn to the towers and a card lists the figures. Drag the card out of the way; its Hide button or the next right-click removes it.
